What we do

Every technique, one place.

DTF Printing
Vibrant, full-colour prints on any fabric. Perfect for detailed designs and photography.
Embroidery
Timeless, premium quality. Ideal for workwear, polos, caps, and branded clothing.
Sublimation
All-over prints that become part of the fabric. Mugs, bags, sportswear and more.
Vinyl
Crisp, bold single-colour designs. Great for names, numbers, and simple logos.
Cards & Invites
Party invites, birthday cards, and flat print keepsakes. Designed and printed locally.
