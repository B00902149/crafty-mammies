What we offer

Five techniques, endless possibilities.

We offer a full range of printing and embroidery methods — all under one roof in Ballyclare. Not sure which is right for you? We'll help you choose.

01

DTF Printing

Direct-to-Film (DTF) printing transfers full-colour designs directly onto fabric using a heat press. Perfect for vibrant, detailed artwork that needs to pop.

  • Full colour — no colour limits
  • Works on cotton, polyester, blends
  • Photographic-quality detail
  • Soft feel on the garment
  • Ideal for: gifts, personalised clothing, logos
Get a DTF quote
02

Embroidery

Machine embroidery stitches your design directly into the fabric. It's the gold standard for professional, premium workwear and branded clothing that lasts.

  • Durable — survives hundreds of washes
  • Premium, professional finish
  • Raised texture adds visual depth
  • Ideal for: polos, jackets, caps, workwear
  • Best for: logos, text, simple designs
Get an embroidery quote
03

Sublimation Printing

Sublimation ink becomes part of the fabric itself, making it impossible to peel or crack. Incredible for all-over prints, mugs, and products that need colour edge to edge.

  • All-over printing possible
  • Colour becomes part of the material
  • Will never peel, crack or fade
  • Ideal for: mugs, bags, sportswear, keepsakes
  • Best on: polyester fabrics and hard surfaces
Get a sublimation quote
04

Vinyl Printing

Heat transfer vinyl is cut and pressed onto fabric. Crisp, bold results for names, numbers, and simple designs — perfect for sports kits and school items.

  • Crisp, clean edges on any design
  • Great for names and numbers
  • Wide range of colours and finishes
  • Ideal for: sports kits, school bags, workwear
  • Best for: simple single-colour designs
Get a vinyl quote
