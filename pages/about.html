Who we are

A mother & daughter, built with love.

Who are we?

We are Lavinia & Nicole — a mother-daughter duo dedicated to delivering high-quality, personalised products with a professional touch. Our business is built on passion, precision, and a commitment to excellence in everything we create.

What do we do?

We do everything you can imagine — from custom embroidery to eye-catching prints! Whether you're looking for a one-of-a-kind jumper, professional workwear that represents your business, or the perfect gift for someone special — we've got you covered.

Where are we?

Come see us in Ballyclare, right on the Square next to Domino's — because who doesn't love a pizza break after a shopping spree? 54 The Square, Ballyclare. Every single item is made in-house, crafted with love by us. No outsourcing — just pure Lavinia & Nicole magic!
